PDF COMPRESSION FAQ

Questions about compressing PDFs

How do I compress a PDF online for free?

Upload a PDF, choose a quality level or target size, then download the smaller file. This version processes the document on the server.

Is my PDF uploaded when I use this compressor?

Yes. The PDF is sent to the server for compression. Temporary processing files are removed after the response.

Can every PDF reach 100 KB or another target size?

No. The result depends on the PDF's page count and images. The compressor returns its smallest result and reports when the target is not reached.
